## Notes

- **Line 1:** Vocables are kept as sung. Translating them would lose the folk rhythm the whole song rests on.
- **Line 2:** "Bhala" is a cheer ("well done"), so it stays as a sound, the way "hey" would in English.
- **Line 4:** "Is one" ends every okkate / okate line, so the English keeps one end rhyme the whole way through, as the Telugu does.
- **Line 12:** The original names the caste (chandala). "Outcast" keeps the point of the verse, that the highest and the lowest stand on the same earth, without the slur.

## Translator's notes

Hari (Vishnu) is kept by name rather than translated to "God": the kirtana is addressed to him, and the name is two syllables that land on two notes.
